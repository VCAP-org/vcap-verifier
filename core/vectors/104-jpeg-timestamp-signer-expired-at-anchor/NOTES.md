# 104-jpeg-timestamp-signer-expired-at-anchor

The same token, and an anchor mined **400 days** after the capture — after the TSA signer's certificate expired (it lives a year). The token's `genTime` is inside that certificate's life and predates the block, so validated at `genTime` alone it holds. But a core first anchored after the certificate expired was stamped, as far as anything can show, by a key that was no longer valid: exactly what a TSA key leaked after its expiry would produce, backdated into the window. With an anchor, the signer must also be valid at the block time (§6.2); without one nothing bounds that risk, and `threat-model.md` §5.4 says so.
